# Too Much Tech — technology survey

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Target:** provisional Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.265 / Java 21. **Mode:** read-only public release research and registry intake. **No mods installed/pinned, no dependency artifacts downloaded, no Minecraft run.**

## Owner direction

- **Core inclusions approved conceptually:** Oritech, Just Dire Things (JDT), Mekanism and Draconic Evolution. They remain *not installed*. Draconic Evolution currently requires the 1.21.1 **beta** release.
- **Thermal Expansion is desired IF AVAILABLE**, but its official upstream files presently stop at **1.20.1**. Thermal Foundation and Dynamics have the same 1.20.1 limitation. HOLD; do not quietly port files or switch Minecraft versions.
- Build for **fun with technology, not GregTech progression**. Complex engineering projects can be rewarding; mandatory repetitive recipe chains, grindy machine-tier gates, and cross-mod processing bureaucracy are not the objective.
- Bring over most of the usable TMM food, QoL and performance tools **after reviewing current artifacts and dependencies**, without copying magic-specific progression or TMM's invalid/stale metadata.

## Main recommendations before a roster freeze

1. **Four-core backbone**: Oritech, JDT, Mekanism (+ Generators; optionally Tools/Additions), Draconic Evolution. Confirm latest exact provider releases, Brandon's Core/CodeChickenLib and Mekanism sibling-module version parity. Test JDT time acceleration on other machine tickers and Draconic energy flows.
2. **Distinct optional factory disciplines**: Create (+ Crafts & Additions bridge), Immersive Engineering and PneumaticCraft. These provide spatial/kinetic/cabling/pressure design rather than another serial machine ladder. Keep them meaningfully accessible and avoid mandatory 'one of every system' chains.
3. **Power as choices, not obligations**: Powah and Extreme Reactors are healthy release candidates, but set sensible OP/FE presentation, upgrade paths and energy-output balance relative to Draconic Evolution before installation. Avoid unbounded FE conversion loops.
4. **Automation**: Industrial Foregoing is attractive but 1.21.1 is **beta**. Productive Bees and Mystical Agriculture have stable releases; decide how renewable resources compete with exploration/mining/ore processing. Modular Routers, LaserIO, Flux Networks and Functional Storage are strong utilities.
5. **Storage**: AE2 19.2.20 and Refined Storage 2.0.9 are both available, but whether to run one/both is a gameplay and performance decision, not a compatibility mandate. Choose early storage philosophy; avoid duplicating long questlines.
6. **Advanced options**: Ender IO is beta; Ender IO: Evolution is a different released project. Choose one *after* evaluating lineage/content. Integrated Dynamics/Tunnels and CC:Tweaked make complex logic and programming **optional**. XNet, Pipez and RFTools 1.21.1 lines are beta candidates.
7. **Exploration/endgame**: Ad Astra 1.16.26 (NeoForge 1.21.1 release) adds a technology-themed planetary dimension arc. Review transport, fuels, chunk loading, boss/worldgen interplay and server costs before making it required.
8. **Thermal**: official Thermal Expansion/Foundation/Dynamics show no 1.21.1 file. FACTAS 0.2.1 is an unrelated beta mod *inspired by Thermal*, not a Thermal Series release or direct port. Leave the real Thermal trilogy on hold.

## Architecture and compatibility gates

- **JDT time acceleration:** accelerated Oritech/Create/Mekanism/Draconic/multiblocks/fluids/nuclear reactors may produce inconsistent state, infinite resources, power spikes, excessive TPS cost or crashes. Verify blacklist/whitelist configs and test controlled examples rather than allowing acceleration universally.
- **Power unification:** choose UI/display of FE/OP and any conversions at a single owner. Draconic Evolution's OP behaves as an FE-compatible energy unit in many setups, but exact version APIs/UI across Jade, CC:Tweaked and machine networks require checks. Don't create conversion loops that profit without an intended mechanic.
- **Ores/processing:** Mekanism tiered yields, Oritech processing, Create crushing, Immersive alloys and generated ores need centralized tag/ore/dust registry, recipe collisions, preferred outputs and optional conversion paths. The content goal is freedom to pick routes, not mandatory detours through all machines.
- **Storage/performance:** large AE2/RS systems, individual item handling, remote item pipes, beehives and build contraptions need server-friendly default limits/profiling. Preserve each mod's native transport where helpful; no forced universal logistics network.
- **Worldgen:** pin/merge worldgen once, avoid duplicate ores and mutually incompatible structures/biomes, and test cross-dimensional pipes, chunkloading and Ad Astra interactions.
- **Modules and version lock:** Oritech, Mekanism and addons need version-matched release checks, libraries, client/server side rules, real SHA hashes and redistribution policy before packwiz indexes change.
- **Quest philosophy:** teach *how* to build working processing lines and optional factories; don't force every decorative tier or crafting-chain step. Complex late-game prestige projects (reactors, fusion, Draconic) may be challenging without being repetitive.

Choose based on distinctive play, not mod count.
