Keep it `held` and `unselected_biotech_survey`; don't create a bogus 1.21.1 pin or change the pack target. Recheck official releases when a 1.21.1 port appears.

This adds **one separately held version-blocked record** beyond the 24 compatible-version candidates below.

## Executive finding

Biotech can be a genuine fourth **technology sector** in TMT (not automatically a fourth player class):

1. **Biological augmentation**: install genetic powers and engineered traits, independent of robotic implants.
2. **Industrial bioengineering**: clone/culture organisms, produce biomaterials, use microbes and organisms as factory components.
3. **Engineered biodiversity**: breed creatures and crop genomes, make unusual pets/farms, possibly design alien ecology.
4. **Medical science**: diagnostic labs, culture handling and treatment/containment systems.
5. **Organic machinery**: flesh-powered machines/serums, a biopunk alternative to metal mechanisms.

The most immediately distinctive choices are **Genetics: Resequenced**, **Improved Genetics**, **Create: Biotech**, **Biotech**, **Symbiont**, **BioForge**, **Biomancy (one port)** and **Forestry CE + Gendustry**. Different genetic content is not a reason to exclude any of these; each deserves actual runtime compatibility tests.

## Primary release checks & actual systems

- [Genetics: Resequenced](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/genetics-resequenced) has official 1.21.1 NeoForge **1.15.0-build.51**, 80+ genetic powers for players and mobs. Its author says **EMI is the supported recipe viewer**, while TMT targets JEI; plan to test EMI/JEI coexistence or custom JEI recipe display rather than assume vanilla recipe UI works.
- [Improved Genetics](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/improved-genetics) has **0.8.1** on 1.21.1 NeoForge. It models individual living-entity genomes and edited offspring through machinery. License shown as **CC BY-NC-ND** on Modrinth: verify distribution and changes rather than assume resource files may be freely redistributed.
- [Create: Biotech](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-biotech) 1.21.1 NeoForge **2.0.0** was published **2026-11-19**; biological machine parts integrate with Create logistics/stress. Its 2.0.0 file notes a **Ponderer Lib 0.1.1** requirement for the CurseForge distribution. Verify exact packaged dependencies.
- [Biotech](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/biotech) has **2.5 release** and **2.7-alpha.3** for 1.21.1 NeoForge. Focus on captured livestock processing, inherited genetic states, breeding chambers, greenhouse and FE/fluid machines; use stable 2.5 as research reference unless alpha features need review.
- [Symbiont](https://modrinth.com/mod/symbiont) has a 1.21.1 NeoForge beta listing; cultivated living cells generate resource outputs, using genetically modified traits and nutrient inputs. Project explicitly **does not** contain energy/logistics generation—suits our already-selected tech.
- [BioForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/bioforge) 2.2 is a 1.21.1 NeoForge medical/pathology laboratory with culture, diagnosis and treatments. Unlike a simple status debuff, this introduces transmissible and persistent pathogen systems; isolate infection and cleanup behavior before combined testing.
- [Forestry: CE](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/forestry-community-edition) 1.21.1-3.0.0-alpha9 has a NeoForge file, with [Gendustry CE Unofficial Port](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/gendustry-ce-unofficial-port) 1.0.10 and Patchouli for advanced bee/tree/butterfly breeding and genetic template replication. Forestry is a **real dependency** for Gendustry; this is an addon relationship, not competing implementations.
- **Biomancy port choice:** [Biomancy Reforged](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/biomancy-reforged) 2.9.11.0 versus [Biomancy (Unofficial Port)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/biomancy-port) 2.9.8.10. Both independently repackage the original flesh-technology gameplay in NeoForge 1.21.1 and likely share registry namespace/mod ID. This is a *real potential technical conflict*, unlike mere gameplay overlap. [Bio-Factory port](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/biofactory-port) depends on Create plus a compatible Biomancy implementation; verify which one.
- [Biomation](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/biomation) 0.1.0 alpha advertises living automation, cloning, microbes and polymerization. Its author explicitly warns that saves may change—isolated experiment, not gameplay accepted.
- [Chimera Lab](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/chimera-lab) 1.0.0 introduces **24 genes**, 3-minute mob transformation, data-driven mod-mob profiles and genetics labs. Could dramatically affect player anatomy and armor/UI; test next to NeoSync, Cybernetics, Matter Overdrive and our deferred Progression.
- [PatientZero](https://modrinth.com/project/patientzero) uses sequencers, samples, custom virtual pathogens, vaccines and optional CC:Tweaked; [Micro Mayhem](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/micromayhem) 2.5.5 describes world-wide microscopic ecosystems and calls itself **highly WIP**; both require strict event-spread rate limits, performance and server policy review.
- [Cloning Bug](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cloning-bug) is a beta self-replicating inventory parasite that doubles as item-duplication technology. Test container boundaries, stack/entity explosion and abuse before introducing it to a live economy. Duplication is the mechanic, not proof of a software bug.
- [The Galaxy Comes Alive](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tgca-avaliproject) 1.4.1 offers playable alien races (Avali, Protogen, Sergal) and creatures. It contributes to a **future Alien form** rather than industrial biotechnology; assess visual/model/physics compatibility with NeoSync and other transformations.

## Initial QA and compatibility plan

1. **Mod/loader identities:** confirm exact 1.21.1 NeoForge artifacts, full dependency trees, mod IDs and redistribution licenses. In particular choose a single original **Biomancy binary implementation**, not both ports; keep Gendustry + Forestry as a proper addon pair.
2. **Persistent player state:** genetics traits, morphs, augmented bodies, NeoSync shells, android forms, death/resurrection, relog and dimension travel. Duplicate gene application and lost state are correctness issues; parallel upgrade mechanics are *not* by themselves.
3. **Entity genetics:** every captured/modified mob carries correct NBT/data components after capture, breeding, death, chunk unload and transfer through Create/Industrial Foregoing systems. Test cross-mod DNA/sampling against hostile and passive modded mobs.
4. **Bioprocessing:** validate fluids/items/FE, Create rotation, gene tubes, native configs, tags and recipe viewers. Check new biomass/food production against existing Pam's, Farmer's Delight and Productive Bees but don't prune for overlap.
5. **Contagion safeguards:** contamination spreads only where intended; test rates in claimed chunks, offline worlds, servers with multiple players, across dimensions; examine per-tick scanning cost. Escape/cleanup controls must work.
6. **Duplication economy:** Cloning Bug and gene replicators can create resources; check expected rates and worldentity/inventory safety with ProjectE-like systems only if actually selected for this pack.
7. **Quality gate:** tiny new mods, alpha releases and experimental ports go to **isolated disposable worlds** before a combined release candidate. Don't confuse a listed provider version with one successful start-up, nor with in-world acceptance.
